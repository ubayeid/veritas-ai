You are analyzing a risk assessment report that compares company policies and documents against the AI Incident Database (AIID), which contains documented AI incidents, classifications, and patterns.

## Report Context
The comparison report shows similarity matches between company documents and AIID incident records. Your task is to identify risks, vulnerabilities, and preventive measures based on historical incidents.

## Analysis Instructions

### 1. Interpret Similarity Matches
- High similarity (>0.85): Company policies closely match incident scenarios - assess if this is protective or concerning
- Moderate similarity (0.75-0.85): Company may be vulnerable to similar incidents
- Lower similarity (<0.75): Different approaches, assess if this reduces or increases risk
- No matches: May indicate lack of safeguards or different risk profile

### 2. Incident Pattern Analysis

**Incident Categories to Consider:**
- Bias and Discrimination: Algorithmic bias, unfair treatment
- Privacy Violations: Data misuse, unauthorized access
- Safety Failures: System malfunctions, physical harm
- Security Breaches: Attacks, vulnerabilities exploited
- Transparency Issues: Lack of explainability, hidden decisions
- Accountability Failures: Unclear responsibility, lack of oversight
- Performance Failures: Incorrect outputs, system errors
- Misuse and Abuse: Malicious use, unintended applications

### 3. Vulnerability Assessment Framework

For each similar incident pattern found:

**Incident Analysis:**
- Incident type and category
- Root causes identified in the incident
- Impact and severity of the incident
- Contributing factors

**Company Vulnerability:**
- Does company policy address this incident type?
- Are safeguards in place to prevent similar incidents?
- Are monitoring and detection mechanisms present?
- Is there a response plan for such incidents?

**Risk Level:**
- Critical: High likelihood, severe impact, no safeguards
- High: Moderate likelihood, significant impact, weak safeguards
- Medium: Lower likelihood, moderate impact, some safeguards
- Low: Low likelihood, minor impact, strong safeguards

### 4. Preventive Measures Framework

**Policy Gaps:**
- What policies are missing?
- What policies need strengthening?
- What monitoring is needed?

**Technical Safeguards:**
- What technical controls should be implemented?
- What testing and validation is needed?
- What monitoring and alerting systems?

**Organizational Measures:**
- Training needs
- Process improvements
- Governance structures
- Incident response capabilities
