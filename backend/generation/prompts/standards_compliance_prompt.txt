You are analyzing a compliance comparison report that compares company policies and documents against regulatory standards (specifically GDPR in this case).

## Report Context
The comparison report shows similarity matches between company documents and regulatory standards. Your task is to interpret these matches and provide a comprehensive compliance assessment.

## Analysis Instructions

### 1. Interpret Similarity Matches
- High similarity scores (>0.85): Strong alignment with standards
- Moderate similarity (0.75-0.85): Partial alignment, may need strengthening
- Lower similarity (<0.75): Potential gaps or different approaches
- No matches: Significant gaps that need attention

### 2. Compliance Assessment Areas

**Data Protection & Privacy:**
- How company policies address data subject rights
- Consent mechanisms and lawful basis for processing
- Data minimization and purpose limitation
- Data retention and deletion policies

**Security Measures:**
- Technical and organizational measures
- Data breach notification procedures
- Security incident response

**Transparency & Accountability:**
- Privacy notices and transparency
- Documentation requirements
- Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)

**Rights of Data Subjects:**
- Right to access, rectification, erasure
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing
- Automated decision-making provisions

### 3. Gap Analysis Framework

For each identified gap:
- **Gap Description**: What specific requirement is missing or insufficient
- **Regulatory Reference**: Which GDPR article or standard clause applies
- **Risk Level**: Critical, High, Medium, or Low
- **Business Impact**: Potential consequences (fines, reputation, operations)
- **Recommendation**: Specific action to address the gap

### 4. Strengths Identification

Also identify:
- Areas where company exceeds minimum requirements
- Best practices demonstrated
- Strong policy provisions that could be models for other areas

## Output Structure

### Executive Summary
- Overall compliance posture (Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Non-Compliant, Unknown)
- Number of strong alignments vs gaps
- Top 3 priority areas

### Detailed Compliance Analysis

#### Strong Alignments
[List areas with high similarity matches]
- Policy section: [reference]
- Standard requirement: [GDPR article/clause]
- Alignment strength: [High/Moderate]
- Assessment: [why this is good]

#### Identified Gaps
[List areas with low/no matches]
- Missing requirement: [specific standard requirement]
- Regulatory reference: [GDPR article]
- Risk level: [Critical/High/Medium/Low]
- Business impact: [potential consequences]
- Recommendation: [specific action]

#### Areas Needing Strengthening
[List moderate alignments]
- Current state: [what exists]
- Standard requirement: [what's needed]
- Gap: [difference]
- Recommendation: [how to strengthen]

### Risk Assessment Summary
- Critical risks: [count and brief description]
- High risks: [count and brief description]
- Medium risks: [count and brief description]
- Low risks: [count and brief description]

### Prioritized Action Plan
1. [Immediate actions - Critical risks]
2. [Short-term actions - High risks]
3. [Medium-term actions - Medium risks]
4. [Long-term actions - Low risks and improvements]

### Compliance Score
Provide an overall compliance score (0-100) with justification.
